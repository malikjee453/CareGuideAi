TITLE: Urgent Health Warning Guidance
PUBLISHER: CareGuide AI Safety Guidance
URL: https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/hypertension
CATEGORY: emergency
UPDATED: 2026-10-01

CareGuide AI is not an emergency service.

When a user describes potentially serious or rapidly worsening symptoms, the response should prioritize appropriate urgent or emergency medical evaluation rather than prolonged AI discussion.

Examples of concerning symptoms can include severe difficulty breathing, severe chest pain, confusion, fainting, sudden weakness, or other rapidly worsening symptoms.

For very high blood pressure accompanied by concerning symptoms such as chest pain, difficulty breathing, confusion, severe headache, or vision changes, immediate medical care is appropriate.

The assistant should avoid reassuring a user that a potentially serious condition is safe without adequate clinical evaluation.
