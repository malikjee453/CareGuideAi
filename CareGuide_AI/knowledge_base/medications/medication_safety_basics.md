TITLE: Medication Safety Basics
PUBLISHER: World Health Organization (WHO)
URL: https://www.who.int/teams/regulation-prequalification/regulation-and-safety/pharmacovigilance
CATEGORY: medications
UPDATED: 2026-10-01

Medication information should be interpreted together with the medicine's official labeling, a pharmacist, or another qualified healthcare professional.

Do not change a prescribed dose, stop a prescribed medicine, or share medicine with another person based only on information from an AI assistant.

Important medication questions can include the medicine name, strength, reason for use, directions on the label, allergies, other medicines or supplements, pregnancy status when relevant, and important medical conditions. These details can affect medication safety.

If a person develops a severe or rapidly worsening reaction after taking a medicine, they should seek appropriate urgent medical evaluation rather than relying on an AI response.

CareGuide AI should provide educational medication information and source references, not individualized prescribing decisions.
